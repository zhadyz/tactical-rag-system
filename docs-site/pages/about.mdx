## Why Forge?

Picture this: you're drowning in a sea of PDFs, technical docs, and meeting notes. You need *one* answer. Traditional search gives you 10,000 results. RAG systems hallucinate. Vector databases return vaguely related nonsense.

We called this Forge because that's exactly what it does — takes raw material and **hammers it into something useful**.

### The Anvil

A forge starts with the anvil — the solid foundation where raw metal meets precision force. That's your document corpus. Messy PDFs, sprawling manuals, scattered knowledge bases. Unrefined ore.

When you ask a question, you shouldn't get back "here's 47 possibly relevant chunks." You should get the *right answer*, shaped from the *right document*, at the *right level of detail*. Like a blacksmith striking exactly where it matters, not flailing at hot metal and hoping for the best.

Precision matters when you're dealing with enterprise knowledge, legal docs, or technical specifications. Close enough isn't good enough.

### The Hammer

The hammer is where the real work happens — 14 techniques working in concert to beat raw information into shape. Hybrid search. Neural reranking. HyDE. Multi-query expansion. Semantic chunking. Confidence scoring. Each one a different strike, a different angle.

Modern embeddings and neural reranking let us understand *intent*, not just keywords. You can ask "how do I prevent memory leaks in the inference pipeline?" and get actual answers about memory management — not every doc that mentions the word "pipeline."

14 tools in the forge. Every one of them earns its place.

### The Fire

Every forge needs heat. Ours is GPU acceleration — CUDA-powered inference running at 80-100 tokens per second, turning cold queries into white-hot answers.

Ever tried finding a specific detail in a 500-page compliance manual at 2 AM? That's raw ore sitting in the dark. Forge brings the heat. Melts through the noise. Extracts exactly what you need, when you need it. No more ctrl+F through endless pages. No more "I know we documented this somewhere..."

Speed isn't a feature. It's the fire that makes everything else possible.

### The Temper — "Bend, don't break"

The best blades aren't just hard — they're *tempered*. Heated, cooled, stressed, and tested until they flex without snapping. That's the philosophy here.

When naive retrieval fails, when semantic search drifts, when GPU memory runs out — Forge adapts. Hybrid search with neural reranking. Intelligent chunking. Memory-efficient inference. Query expansion. Graceful degradation at every layer. Five levels of caching so the same question never costs you twice.

**The best tools don't just work. They work when everything else doesn't.**

## The Mission

Look, RAG is everywhere now. But most implementations are... let's be diplomatic and say "suboptimal."

We wanted to build something that:
- **Actually retrieves the right documents** (not just semantically similar ones)
- **Runs fast enough to feel instant** (GPU acceleration, optimized inference)
- **Doesn't hallucinate** (proper grounding, source attribution)
- **Scales to real workloads** (thousands of documents, concurrent users)

Forge is what happens when you stop accepting "good enough" and start demanding "actually good."

This is what information retrieval should have been all along.
